# Graph Metrics

Each experiment records where applicable: num_nodes, num_edges, num_similarity_edges, num_dependency_edges, avg_degree, graph_density, num_communities, largest_community, avg_similarity, max_similarity, and connected_components.

Additional measures can include per-model node counts, model diversity per community, centrality distributions, community size distribution, and similarity quantiles.

Graph statistics are descriptive, not proof of answer quality. Dense graphs may reflect redundancy or permissive thresholds; fragmented graphs may reflect diversity, embedding mismatch, or strict thresholds. Do not label communities as semantic strategies without independent validation.

# Research Positioning

## Existing concepts
Ensemble reasoning, multi-sample reasoning, embedding similarity, graph ranking, community detection, PageRank, eigenvector centrality, clustering, and centroid representatives are established concepts. No novelty claim is made without literature verification.

## Engineering contribution
This framework consumes frozen candidate trajectories, supports graph and answer-level baselines, exposes YAML parameters, standardizes result records, runs ablations, preserves historical artifacts, and separates generation from evaluation.

## Potential research contribution
Test whether cross-model structural information adds useful selection signals beyond independent ranking. Stronger claims require controlled benchmark results, ablations, cost analysis, and systematic failure analysis.
